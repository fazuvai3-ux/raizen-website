Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 30, 2026

RAIZEN respects user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used and how users can contact us about their information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use RAIZEN, we may collect:

  • Name and username provided during profile setup.
  • Email address associated with your account.
  • Account authentication information handled through Firebase Authentication.
  • App usage and diagnostic information through Firebase Analytics.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Maintain user profiles.
  • Provide and improve RAIZEN services.
  • Understand app usage and improve performance.
  • Respond to support requests.

3. Data Storage and Service Providers

RAIZEN uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Firebase Analytics. Information processed by these services may be handled by Google according to the applicable service terms and privacy documentation.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the platform, or disclosed when required by applicable law.

5. Data Security

We use available security features and access controls to help protect user information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and Account Requests

Account information may be retained while an account is active and for as long as necessary for legitimate operational or legal purposes. Users can contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion requests.

7. Children's Privacy

RAIZEN is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. Where required, appropriate parental consent and safeguards will be applied.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when RAIZEN's services or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
